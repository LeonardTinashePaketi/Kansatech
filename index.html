[03] ABOUT

Built on craft,
driven by service.

Kansatech (Pvt) Ltd is an independent electrical, mechanical, refrigeration & air conditioning (HVAC) company operating across Harare, Zimbabwe (Main Branch) and Midrand, South Africa (South Africa Branch). We are committed to delivering excellence across every project — from a single service call to a major construction contract.

Our mission: provide satisfactory services, build long-term client relationships through high-quality work and support, and grow sustainably while creating jobs and boosting the economy.

VALUES
Compassion · Integrity · Honesty · Professionalism
